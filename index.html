About Genghis Khan

Genghis Khan, born Temujin, united the Mongol tribes and built the largest land empire in history. Life under his rule was harsh but highly organized, with an advanced communication system and surprising religious tolerance.

"I am the flail of God. If you had not committed great sins, God would not have sent a punishment like me upon you."
